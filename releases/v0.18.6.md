# Housekeeping 0.18.6

Housekeeping can now stay in the menu bar with its Dock icon hidden.

## Show in Dock

Open **Settings → Startup → Show in Dock**. Turn it off to remove Housekeeping from the Dock and Command-Tab while keeping the menu-bar octopus available. The change applies immediately and is remembered across relaunches. Turn it back on to restore the Dock icon.

Existing windows remain usable when switching modes. The menu-bar item is installed before the saved Dock preference is applied at launch. Dock visibility stays on by default, and the separate Open at Login setting is unchanged.

This is a small update to [0.18.5](https://github.com/aghamorad/Housekeeping/releases/tag/v0.18.5), which introduced the refined burgundy interface, repaired Platinum layout, improved storage sorting, and optional local chat model choices.

## Downloads

- **Housekeeping-macOS-universal.zip:** Apple silicon and Intel in one app.
- **Housekeeping-macOS-arm64.zip:** Apple-silicon Macs only.
- **Housekeeping-macOS-x86_64.zip:** Intel Macs only.
- **SHA256SUMS.txt:** checksums for all three ZIPs.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Apple Intelligence chat additionally requires an eligible Mac running macOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence enabled and its system model ready. Optional downloadable model weights are not included in the ZIPs.

The app is ad-hoc signed. Developer ID signing and notarization have not been completed.
